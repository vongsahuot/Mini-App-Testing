Mini App Payment Flow

How a customer opens a partner mini app from the bank app's native mini apps list, signs in, pays, and gets refunded if needed, across the bank app, KOSIGN Portal, the partner and the bank's payment API. Each phase is drawn on its own; lanes not used in a phase are faded. Steps outlined in green are new or changed in this version.

Key rules
  • Bridges work only on the partner's allowlisted origins.
  • authCode is created at login(), after consent: one use, 60 s.
  • Price is set on the partner server; a repeated orderId never charges twice.
  • PENDING → PROCESSING happens once; stuck payments are settled by asking the bank.
  • The signed webhook is the source of truth, retried for 24 h; pay() is a UI hint.
  • A daily settlement file reconciles every transaction.
